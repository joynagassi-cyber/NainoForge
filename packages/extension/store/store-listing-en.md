## Detailed Description

NainoForge is a Chrome extension that transforms how you learn and retain information from the web. Using spaced repetition and AI-powered cognitive mapping, it helps you forge lasting knowledge from articles, videos, and web content.

### Key Features

**🧠 Spaced Repetition**
- Intelligent review scheduling based on your mastery level
- Track your progress with streaks and mastery graphs
- Never forget what you've learned

**🕸️ Knowledge Graph (COSMOS)**
- Visualize relationships between concepts
- See your learning progress at a glance
- Identify gaps in your understanding

**🤖 AI-Powered Insights**
- Get summaries and explanations from AI
- Ask questions about captured content
- Review with AI-assisted teach-back

**📥 Content Capture**
- Capture articles, YouTube videos, and any web content
- Automatic extraction and cleaning
- Rich text editing with BlockNote

**🎯 Review Surface**
- Clean, focused review interface
- Confidence tracking for each concept
- Progress visualization

### How to Use

1. **Capture**: Click the NainoForge icon on any webpage to capture content
2. **Forge**: Review and edit the extracted content in IMPRINT
3. **Connect**: View and manage your knowledge graph in COSMOS
4. **Review**: Practice spaced repetition in the Review surface
5. **Learn**: Use Student AI for teach-back and reinforcement

### Use Cases

- **Students**: Master course material from lectures and textbooks
- **Researchers**: Build comprehensive knowledge bases
- **Professionals**: Learn and retain industry knowledge
- **Lifelong learners**: Forge expertise in any topic

### Privacy

- No data collection - everything stays on your device
- No external servers or tracking
- Your knowledge is yours alone
