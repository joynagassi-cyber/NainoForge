# Privacy Policy for NainoForge

**Last Updated: August 1, 2026**

## Overview

NainoForge is a Chrome extension that helps users forge cognitive concepts through spaced repetition and knowledge graph visualization. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Collect

### Local Data (Stored on Your Device)
- **Captured content**: Articles, videos, and web pages you choose to capture
- **Knowledge graph**: Your personal concept maps and relationships
- **Spaced repetition data**: Review schedules and mastery levels
- **Session history**: Your learning sessions and progress

### No Data Sent to Servers
- We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers
- All your data stays locally in your browser's storage
- No analytics or tracking

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide the core functionality of content capture and knowledge management
- To power the spaced repetition algorithm for optimal learning
- To visualize your knowledge graph
- To provide AI-powered insights (processed locally or through your configured AI provider)

## Permissions Explanation

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `activeTab` | Access current tab content for capture |
| `sidePanel` | Display the NainoForge interface |
| `storage` | Save your data locally |
| `offscreen` | Process PDF content offscreen |

## Third-Party Services

- **AI Provider**: If you configure an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), your prompts are sent directly to that provider. We do not intercept or store your conversations.
- **PDF.js**: Used for PDF processing, runs entirely in your browser.

## Data Security

- All data is stored using Chrome's secure storage API
- No data is transmitted to our servers (we don't have any)
- Your data is accessible only through this extension

## Children's Privacy

NainoForge is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the extension's settings or through our GitHub repository.
